# Beauty & Wellness Data Science Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains a data science project dedicated to addressing critical business challenges in the **Beauty & Wellness sector**. Combining comprehensive marketing insights with predictive analytics, this project frames a real-world business issue, performs advanced exploratory data analysis, and proposes an analytical framework to optimize customer experiences and business outcomes.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem Statement
### **"Optimizing Customer Retention & Personalization via Trend Forecasting and Recommendation Systems"**
In the fast-evolving Beauty & Wellness industry, consumer preferences shift rapidly due to social media trends and changing product sentiments. Traditional static marketing fails to retain highly dynamic consumers. 

**Core Problem:** Brands struggle to anticipate emerging micro-trends and personalize product recommendation pipelines, leading to high customer churn and inefficient ad spend.

---

## 🔬 Research Questions & Hypotheses
*   **Research Question 1:** Can historical purchase cycles and social media sentiment accurately forecast upcoming product micro-trends 30 days in advance?
    *   *Hypothesis 1:* Integrating real-time sentiment metrics into traditional time-series data improves trend forecasting accuracy by over 15%.
*   **Research Question 2:** How effectively can user behavior profiles and ingredient-based matching mitigate the 'cold start' recommendation problem for newly launched wellness items?
    *   *Hypothesis 2:* Hybrid filtering (combining collaborative filtering with content/ingredient filtering) yields higher click-through rates (CTR) for new products compared to collaborative filtering alone.

---

## 📊 Variables & Data Sources
To investigate this problem, the framework utilizes the following datasets and variables:
*   **Customer Transaction Data:** Customer ID, purchase timestamp, order frequency, basket value, and product category.
*   **Product Catalog Metadata:** Brand, skin/hair type labels, ingredient list, and pricing tier.
*   **Sentiment & Web Data:** Textual reviews, product star ratings, and macro social media trend engagement metrics.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Analytical Approach
1. **Industry Research & Trend Mapping:** Review public market intelligence reports and academic literature detailing shifting pillars in beauty e-commerce.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Identify purchase patterns, seasonality variations, missing data structures, and demographic distributions.
3. **Feature Engineering & Selection:** Construct customer lifetime value indicators, aggregate review sentiments using Natural Language Processing (NLP), and parse raw ingredient lists into functional attributes.
4. **Predictive Modeling:**
    *   *Trend Prediction:* **Time-Series Models** (e.g., ARIMA, Prophet).
    *   *Recommendation Framework:* **Hybrid Recommendation Engines** (LightFM / Matrix Factorization).
5. **Business Evaluation:** Translate statistical outcomes into actionable marketing insights (e.g., target customer groups and localized inventory planning).
